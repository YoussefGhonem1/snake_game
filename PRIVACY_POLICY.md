# Privacy Policy for Snakes Game

**Last Updated: October 9, 2025**

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Snakes Game" mobile application ("App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

**Important:** This App does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All game data is stored locally on your device.

---

## Information We Collect

### Game Data (Stored Locally Only)

All of the following information is stored exclusively on your device using local storage (SharedPreferences):

- **Game Progress**: Current level, unlocked levels, and completion status
- **High Scores**: Your best scores and achievements
- **Game Settings**: Language preference, control settings, and game preferences
- **User Coins**: Virtual currency earned through gameplay
- **Snake Customizations**: Selected snake designs and owned snake skins
- **Game Statistics**: Total games played, total score, longest snake achieved

**No data is transmitted to external servers or third parties.**

---

## How We Use Your Information

### Game Functionality

- **Local Storage**: All game data is stored locally on your device and never leaves your device
- **Game Features**: Your locally stored data enables features like:
  - Level progression and unlocking
  - Score tracking and leaderboard (local only)
  - Customization options
  - Language localization
- **Offline Play**: Since all data is local, you can play the game entirely offline

---

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only

- **Device-Only Storage**: 100% of your game data remains on your device
- **No Cloud Sync**: We do not sync your game data to external servers
- **No Accounts**: The App does not require or support user accounts
- **Data Persistence**: Your progress is automatically saved locally between sessions

### Security Measures

- **Local Encryption**: Game data is stored securely using Android/iOS native storage mechanisms
- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal identification information
- **No Network Access for Data**: The App does not send your game data over the internet

---

## Third-Party Services

**We do not use any third-party services for analytics, advertising, or data collection.**

The App operates completely independently without:
- Analytics services
- Advertising networks
- Cloud storage services
- Social media integrations
- User tracking services

## Your Rights and Control

### Data Management

- **View Your Data**: All your data is stored in the App's local storage on your device
- **Delete Your Data**: 
  - Clear game progress through the in-app settings, or
  - Completely remove all data by uninstalling the App
- **No Data Requests Needed**: Since we don't collect or store data on our servers, there's no need to request data deletion from us

### Privacy Settings

- **No Tracking**: The App does not track your behavior or collect usage analytics
- **No Permissions Required**: The App only requests permissions essential for gameplay (if any)
- **No Notifications**: The App does not send push notifications
